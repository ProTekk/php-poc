## The SAPI nuance (cleanup-regime-dependent, patch-independent)

- **The RCE leg needs no cross-request artifact**: the pubstate gate fires on the
  published state itself (progress field passes through the app), stock
  `cleanup=1` included. This is why it survives the vendor patch intact — the
  shipped fix targets OOM corruption and stale globals, neither of which this
  chain consumes.
- **The cross-request staleness artifact** (the abandoned-upload frozen counter
  read in a later request) is **prefork-native** (`cleanup=0` venues, .16-style)
  and on FPM **requires `cleanup=0`**: FPM completes the abandoned request's
  lifecycle and stock cleanup wipes the entry. `cleanup=0` is an ini-permitted
  setting, not a patch dependency.
